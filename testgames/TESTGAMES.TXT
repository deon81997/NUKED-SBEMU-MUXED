NUKED SB-EMU - FREE TEST GAMES
==============================

Ten shareware games to try the pack with. Each file is the publisher's own,
untouched shareware archive. The first five come from the 1994 "Super
Shareware Collection" CD (only the CD's "_" was dropped from three file
names); the five General MIDI games keep their original download names.
They are shareware: if you like one, the full game is sold today by its
rights holder.

They are offered here as separate free downloads, exactly as their own
licence files allow for online services and BBSes. They are NOT part of the
NUKED SB-EMU pack and are not covered by its GPL licence. Do not sell them,
and pass them on only as the complete, unmodified ZIP files.

  PINBALL.ZIP   Epic Pinball (Epic MegaGames)          SB / SB Pro / GUS
                digital music + sound: SBEMU's Sound Blaster, DMA and mixing
  ELECTRO.ZIP   Electro Man (Epic MegaGames / XLAND)   SB + AdLib music
                OPL3 (FM) music next to Sound Blaster effects
  HEART.ZIP     Heartlight (Epic MegaGames / XLAND)    SB + AdLib music
                a light game for slow PCs; joystick
  GOT100.ZIP    God of Thunder (Software Creations)    SB effects + AdLib music
                a 286 game: real-mode sound (needs JEMM + QPIEMU, SC55JEM)
  BATS20.ZIP    Invasion of the Mutant Space Bats      Sound Blaster, joystick
                of Doom (Pop Software)

General MIDI games - for the SC-55 (MIDI port 330h):
  3dduke13.zip  Duke Nukem 3D 1.3D shareware (3D Realms) GM music + SB effects
                Bobby Prince's MIDI score, fast timer (TIMETEST / SBDIAG)
  1rott13.zip   Rise of the Triad: The HUNT Begins 1.3  GM music + SB effects
                (Apogee) - same sound library as Duke 3D, older version
  1rap12.zip    Raptor: Call of the Shadows 1.2         GM music + SB effects
                (Cygnus Studios / Apogee) - easy shooter, good long test
  htic_v12.zip  Heretic 1.2 shareware (Raven / id)      GM music + SB effects
                same sound code as Doom (DMX)
  desc14sw.exe  Descent 1.4 shareware (Parallax /       GM music (HMI driver)
                Interplay) - a ZIP that unzips itself; 3D with heavy CPU use

How to use:
  1. Copy the ZIP to the PC (or a USB stick with /USBDISK:1, or a CD image).
  2. Unzip it into a folder, e.g. C:\GAMES\PINBALL (PKUNZIP or 7-Zip).
     The Apogee / 3D Realms / id / Descent archives hold an installer:
     unzip into a temporary folder and run INSTALL (Heretic: INSTALL.BAT).
  3. Start the SC-55 (SC55, or SC55JEM for real-mode games), run the game's
     setup and pick Sound Blaster at 220h, the IRQ / DMA SBEMU shows
     (SBSET or the BLASTER line), and AdLib / OPL music where offered.
  4. Or add it to MENU > Games so the menu starts it with the right
     settings. For MIDI music pick "General MIDI" (or "Sound Canvas") at
     port 330h.
